Project Goal

Make YouTube metadata work manageable at playlist scale.

YouTube Genie is a lightweight browser tool for creators and channel operators who need to review, export, edit, and update many video titles and descriptions without stepping through YouTube Studio one video at a time.

Why it exists

Playlist maintenance often becomes repetitive: fixing titles, cleaning descriptions, applying consistent language, or preparing a batch of edits for review. The project turns that workflow into a focused editing surface with import and export support.

How it works

After connecting a Google account, the app loads playlists from the authenticated YouTube channel, lets you edit playlist and video metadata locally, and pushes only the modified items through the YouTube Data API.

Design principles

The app stays small: vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript; no build step; no backend; and JSON files as a portable review format. The goal is a practical tool that can run anywhere static files can be served.
